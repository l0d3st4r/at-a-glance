At A Glance · Mockup

Dark mode

The real Week, Game and Game Info pages with a dark theme on top. The new switch sits in the left corner of the bottom bar, opposite the +/− toggle. Flip it in any phone and all three change together. Every phone still swipes, toggles and opens its cards.

The switch

A 48 × 28 pill pinned 16px from the left edge, centered on the same line as the week pill and the +/− toggle. The knob carries a sun or a moon, and it grows on hover and press the same way the +/− does. It shows on every page that has the bottom bar.

Palette

  • Background #0B0B0C
  • Card fill #161618
  • Card outline white 12%
  • Text / 2nd / 3rd #F2F2F2 · 62% · 40%

What changes

Only colors. Helmets and team-color pills stay as they are. Win, loss and injury colors get one step brighter (#4CC76E, #FF6B6B, #E8B93A) so they stay readable on black. The choice would be saved on the device and would follow the phone's own setting until someone flips it.
